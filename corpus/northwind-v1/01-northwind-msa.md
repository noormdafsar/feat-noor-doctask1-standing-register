MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between: Harbour Data Services Ltd ("Supplier") and Northwind Logistics plc ("Customer")

Reference: MSA-2023-0902
Effective date: 1 September 2023

1. TERM

1.1 This Agreement takes effect on 1 September 2023 and continues for an initial term of 36 months.

1.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

2. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

2.1 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

2.2 Invoices are issued monthly in arrears.

3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 The Supplier shall maintain monthly availability of at least 99.9% for the Platform, measured across each calendar month.

3.2 Where availability falls below the committed level the Customer is entitled to a service credit of 10% of the monthly charge for the affected month.

4. LIABILITY

4.1 Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the total charges paid in the 24 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

6. TERMINATION

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 120 days written notice.

7. ASSIGNMENT

7.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

8. GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Scotland.
