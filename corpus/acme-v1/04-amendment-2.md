AMENDMENT NO. 2

Amendment No. 2 to Master Services Agreement MSA-2024-0117

Between Vertex Cloud Systems Ltd and Acme Industrial Supply Ltd
Date of amendment: 4 December 2024
Effective date: 1 January 2025

1. The unit price is increased to GBP 48.50 per licence per month with effect from 1 January 2025.

2. Clause 2.2 is further amended so that the Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 60 days of the invoice date.

3. Clause 1.2 is amended so that the notice period for non-renewal is reduced to 30 days before the end of the then-current term.

4. All other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain unchanged.
