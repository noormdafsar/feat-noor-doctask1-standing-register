MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between: Vertex Cloud Systems Ltd ("Supplier") and Acme Industrial Supply Ltd ("Customer")

Reference: MSA-2024-0117
Effective date: 1 January 2024

1. TERM

1.1 This Agreement takes effect on 1 January 2024 and continues for an initial term of 24 months.

1.2 On expiry of the initial term this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

2. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

2.1 Charges for the Services are those set out in the applicable Order Form.

2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

2.3 Invoices are issued quarterly in advance.

2.4 The Supplier may increase charges once in any twelve month period on not less than sixty days notice.

3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 The Supplier shall maintain monthly availability of at least 99.5% for the Platform, measured across each calendar month.

3.2 Where availability falls below the committed level the Customer is entitled to a service credit of 5% of the monthly charge for the affected month.

4. LIABILITY

4.1 Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the total charges paid in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

4.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other party which is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

6. TERMINATION

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days written notice.

6.2 Either party may terminate immediately for material breach that remains uncured after 30 days written notice.

7. ASSIGNMENT

7.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

8. GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of England and Wales.
